PRIVACY POLICY

Your recordings belong to you.

Effective August 29, 2026

Overview

Creator Capture is designed to create and manage media on your iPhone without requiring an account or an external Creator Capture service. The current app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud accounts, or third-party SDKs. Creator Capture does not transmit recordings or personal information to the developer.

Screen recording and ReplayKit

Creator Capture uses Apple’s ReplayKit Broadcast Upload Extension to receive screen video and the audio streams that iOS makes available during a broadcast. Microphone audio is optional and controlled in Apple’s broadcast picker. Captured media is written locally to an App Group container shared only by the Creator Capture app and its recording extension. Apple and iOS may restrict capture of protected content.

Microphone

With your permission, microphone access is used when you enable it for a screen broadcast, record a teleprompter camera video, or create an Audio Commentary take. Audio is recorded into the media file for the feature you started. Creator Capture does not listen or record in the background for analytics or advertising.

Camera

With your permission, Creator Capture uses the front or back camera only for teleprompter video recording. Camera frames are recorded locally into the video you create and are not sent to the developer.

Photo library and selected files

Creator Capture requests add-only Photos access only when you choose Save to Photos. For Audio Commentary, you choose a video through Apple’s file picker. The app uses that selected file for local playback and does not upload it to the developer.

Local recordings

Screen recordings and saved teleprompter videos are stored locally in Creator Capture’s App Group container. Audio Commentary takes are also stored locally. They remain until you delete them, remove the app and its data, or manage them through an Apple system action. You control whether to save a copy to Photos.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Share / Save, Creator Capture presents Apple’s system share sheet. Any transmission then depends on the destination and service you choose. Those third parties have their own privacy practices. Creator Capture does not automatically share media.

Data collection and tracking

The current version does not collect data from the app, create user profiles, use advertising identifiers, or track you across apps and websites. If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Creator Capture is a general-purpose media tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to floodtechs@me.com. Please do not email private recordings.
