How can we help?

Find quick answers for recording, permissions, saving, and sharing.

Screen recording

Tap the record icon in Creator Capture, select Creator Capture Recorder in Apple’s broadcast picker, choose whether the microphone is on, then tap Start Broadcast. Stop from the iPhone recording indicator. Return to the app and refresh the recordings list if needed.

ReplayKit controls what iOS permits an app to capture. Some protected video or audio may not be recordable.

Camera and microphone permissions

Teleprompter recording requires Camera and Microphone access. Audio Commentary requires Microphone access. If access was denied, open iPhone Settings, find Creator Capture, and enable the permission you want to use.

Photos and files

Save to Photos requires permission to add media to your photo library. Choosing a video for Audio Commentary may require photo-library access or access to a file you select. Creator Capture only performs these actions when you request them.

Recordings and trimming

Open a recent recording to play it. Set Start and End to preview a range, then save a new trimmed copy. The original is preserved. Use the share button for Apple’s system share sheet or delete recordings you no longer need.

Contact support

For help, email floodtechs@me.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not email private recordings.
